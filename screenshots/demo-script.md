# SignToROSS product demo capture plan

Status: reference preparation only.

Target length: 45 to 60 seconds.

1. Open the local Mike assistant and create a contract from a synthetic brief.
2. Show a tracked revision and a short review note.
3. Hand the finished DOCX to the local OpenSign instance.
4. Open the recipient signing page through the public hostname.
5. Complete the signature with a reserved demo recipient and show the status
   transition.
6. Show the verified callback, signed PDF, and audit trail in the private
   admin surface.
7. End with the tunnel preflight and hardening checks.

Capture rules:

- Use only synthetic documents, names, email addresses, and secrets.
- Do not record real customer documents, credentials, tokens, or local paths.
- Do not imply that an electronic signature is legally sufficient in a given
  jurisdiction. SignToROSS is software, not legal advice or a compliance
  product.
- Keep the public recipient route separate from the protected admin route.
